# Website prospect research - 27 September 2026

Eight US agriculture and local-service business websites were visually reviewed through the browser extension. Five prospects were shortlisted. Start with J&A Lawn & Landscape; Henningson Farms is the strongest agriculture prospect.

## Read the research

- [Shortlist, evidence and outreach drafts](shortlist.md): readable directly on GitHub.
- [Full visual report](research.html): download and open in a browser. Screenshots are embedded so this file works independently.
- [Structured prospect data](prospects.json): five ranked prospects with sources, observations and suggested offers.
- [Original website screenshots](screenshots/): 24 captures supporting the review.

The report includes comparable agency case studies, public business contact details, qualification questions, suggested measurement and hypothetical payback calculations. External source links require internet access.

## Evidence limits

Prospects are not confirmed buyers. Case-study outcomes are agency-reported and do not isolate the effect of design. Narrow desktop screenshots are not physical-phone tests. Forms and checkout transactions were not submitted. No outreach was sent and no custom prospect website was implemented.

This dated research is separate from the older website-redesign project in this repository. It does not replace that project's findings or validate its other prospects.
