# India-relevant competitors: commercial scale and pricing

Checked 15 September 2026. India-first lens. Eight companies reviewed. **None disclosed a verified current count of paying factory customers in the sources examined.** Company-wide revenue, deployments and connected cameras establish activity but cannot be treated as factory-AI recurring revenue.

## How to read the numbers

- **Signed:** executed orders/contracts. A partnership or intended rollout is not automatically booked revenue.
- **Deployed:** installed or operating at sites. A company may own many sites; one site may contain many cameras.
- **ARR:** annualized recurring subscription run rate. It is not revenue earned during the year, cash collected or committed order value.
- **Reported financials:** secondary databases referencing filings. Original accounts were not obtained. Conflicting labels/values are retained as uncertainty rather than silently reconciled.
- **ND:** not disclosed or not verified in this research. It never means zero.

## What can actually be calculated

No defensible factory-market revenue estimate can be derived by multiplying these heterogeneous installation totals by an assumed camera fee. A buyer-side quote comparison can instead use:

**First-year cost = setup + required equipment + 12 × monthly license + processing/storage/transfer + support + tax.**

For each of the eight vendors, request a written quote for the same Indian site: 10 cameras, stated video quality, continuous analysis, exact same two events, retention and alert response requirements. Separate optional fees and year-two renewal. For subscriptions already partly bundled into a starter kit, count only the remaining months. Signed pilot conversion, actual uptime and renewal evidence would be more useful to Kenesis than inflated total-camera comparisons.

Original filings, private quotes and customer invoices were not obtained. This report deliberately leaves unknowns visible.
