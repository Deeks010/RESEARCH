## Revenue calculations where customer counts are missing

**Drishti has real published prices but no verified customer count.** Assume one Advanced subscription per paying factory, all active for a full year, no discounts and no hardware revenue:

| Assumed paying factories | Calculation | Annual subscription value |
|---:|---|---:|
| 10 | 10 × ₹14,999 × 12 | ₹17,99,880, approximately **₹18 lakh** |
| 50 | 50 × ₹14,999 × 12 | ₹89,99,400, approximately **₹90 lakh** |
| 100 | 100 × ₹14,999 × 12 | ₹1,79,98,800, approximately **₹1.80 crore** |

This is a sensitivity calculation, not a claim that Drishti has 10, 50 or 100 customers. Annual discounts, staggered starts and churn reduce recognized sales relative to this full-year model.

**Guardex: neither price nor paying count is established.** Using its institution's 12-pilot figure solely as a starting cohort, assume 25%, 50% or 100% convert, one site each. At an invented ₹3 lakh annual subscription per site, the resulting 3/6/12 paid sites would produce **₹9/18/36 lakh annual subscription value**. At an invented ₹6 lakh/site, results double to **₹18/36/72 lakh**. The pilot count itself conflicts with the narrative; these are planning scenarios, not estimated current Guardex earnings. [Institution evidence](guardex_profile.md)

**viAct: a deployment count exists but the price is missing.** If 300 of its reported worksites were simultaneously paying, each using 20 billed cameras at an assumed $100/camera-month, the result is **300 × 20 × $100 × 12 = $7.2m/year**. At assumed $50/$200 rates it becomes **$3.6m/$14.4m**. Neither the rate nor that paid/full-year scope is verified; this is conditional annual value, not an actual revenue estimate. [Evidence and model](global_commercial_scale.md)

**CompScience: insurance economics.** Approximately $50m premium volume × assumed retained share of 10%/15%/20% gives **$5m/$7.5m/$10m** before expenses. Its actual retained share and premium period are unknown. These results are neither confirmed annual revenue nor factory software earnings. [Evidence](global_commercial_scale.md)

For the remaining unknowns, an individual numeric revenue guess would require inventing both what was sold and the price. The research records third-party estimates separately for inspection, including Intenseye and Avathon, instead of promoting them to verified facts.
