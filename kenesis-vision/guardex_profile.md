# Guardex.ai: competitor briefing for Kenesis

**Assessment: Guardex belongs on Kenesis's closest-competitor shortlist.** It targets Indian factories with existing-CCTV intelligence. Its public evidence supports a real early-stage company and pilot activity, but does not establish recurring revenue or comparative performance.

Checked 15 September 2026. This concerns guardex.ai, not similarly named cybersecurity, guard-service, VPN or industrial-supplies businesses. No company outreach or authenticated product testing was performed.

## Claimed results versus verified results

The homepage claims 80% less manual video scanning, 50% faster detection, three-times-quicker incident response and 40% better resource allocation. It supplies no named customer, baseline, sample or calculation alongside those figures. They remain marketing claims. [Homepage metrics](https://www.guardex.ai/)

No public benchmark for missed events, false-alert workload, cross-camera tracking accuracy or sustained availability was verified. No numerical subscription price, renewal rate or independently confirmed financial return was found in the reviewed sources. This is missing evidence, not evidence of poor performance.

## What this changes for Kenesis

**Move Guardex into the first comparison group for India.** This is our assessment based on buyer, geography and product overlap. A smaller direct rival may be more relevant to early Indian factory buying decisions than a large overseas platform.

The most important overlap is workstation/operator visibility plus factory-specific operations. Kenesis should not claim that this direction is unoccupied. The proposed absence experiment remains valid, but it needs a better buying reason: useful results, practical installation and a price that works for the same customer.

Do not claim Kenesis wins because Guardex lacks falls, requires equipment, cannot scale SOPs or has no paying customers. Public evidence does not establish any of those statements.

## Five questions for a comparable demonstration

1. Which named pilot sites became paid deployments, and can a buyer confirm the result?
2. What is the full first-year and renewal price for the same cameras and events?
3. What computer, network access and installation work are required at the factory?
4. Can the system distinguish an uncovered station from approved breaks, blocked views and legitimate off-camera work?
5. Can the assistant answer a predefined question set with correct clips, timestamps and an honest indication of missing coverage?

Use the same footage and definitions for Kenesis and Guardex. Compare correct alerts, missed events, setup effort and resulting factory actions. That would establish relative performance; the public research cannot.

## Source limitations

The strongest independent-of-vendor sources are an incubator newsletter and educational-institution reports. These corroborate identity and engagement but are still promotional ecosystem publications, not audited customer studies. Some PDFs were available only through indexed text. Publication and crawl dates were not treated as interchangeable. No outreach was sent, no forms submitted and no private account accessed.
